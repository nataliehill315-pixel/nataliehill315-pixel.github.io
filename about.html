My story

I came to Nebraska in 2024 to study journalism and haven't slowed down since. I started as a news reporter at The Daily Nebraskan, pitching and chasing stories every week, and I've since worked my way up to assistant news editor and now senior news editor, deciding what our campus needs to know and helping reporters tell it well.

What keeps me hooked is that journalism is really about people. Whether I'm interviewing a city official, sitting down with a student, or cutting a script for the radio, my job is to understand someone's point of view and represent it fairly. I pair that with my advertising & PR coursework, so I think about not just what a story says but who needs to hear it and how it reaches them.

Right now I'm balancing the newsroom, a news role at Nebraska Public Media, and my work as a college ambassador, all while keeping my grades up.
