Car Sweeper

Class starts in 10 minutes and you still need somewhere to park. Some spots on campus are fair game, and others are a guaranteed ticket. Find every legal spot in the lot! Pull into a spot to check it out. If it's legal, a number tells you how many of the 8 spots around it will get you ticketed. Read the clues carefully and you'll never have to gamble on a spot.

Know a spot is a ticket waiting to happen? Set out a traffic cone so nobody pulls in by mistake. The red counter shows how many ticket spots are still unmarked. Once a number has that many cones around it, pull into the number again to park in every other spot next to it at once.
