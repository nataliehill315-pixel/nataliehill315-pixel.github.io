Who in the World Is UNL's Next Chancellor?

UNL needs a new chancellor, and you've been appointed to the search committee. Walk across campus, talk to people and hold listening sessions to find out what kind of leader campus wants and where to look next. Word is the best candidate in the pool is secretly scouting campus before deciding whether to stay in the race. Use the search portal to figure out who it is, then track them down and convince them to stay before the Board of Regents meets Friday at 5 p.m.

Every action takes time. If nobody at a stop has heard anything about the search, you've wandered off the trail, so head back. When someone tells you to hurry because the candidate is on campus right now, the next stop is the last one. Figure out who you're looking for, go there and investigate to find them.
