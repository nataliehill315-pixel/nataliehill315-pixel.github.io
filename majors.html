How many students are in your major?

Search any UNL major to see how enrollment has changed every fall since 2016.

    About the data. Headcount of students enrolled in each major each fall, from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln (records request DR12573). Students are counted under each major they declare, so a double major appears in both. Majors are matched by the name the university used each year: a program that was renamed, merged or split appears as separate entries, and a gap in a line means the major was not listed that fall. Undergraduate and graduate programs that share a name are added together; use “By college & degree” to separate them. The “Business Administration” college is shown as “Business,” its later name.
