How the track record works. The data records each prediction (shadow = six more weeks of winter, no shadow = early spring) but not the actual weather, so a predictor's score is the share of years it agreed with the majority of all predictors that year. Years with an even split or no recorded call are left out. It measures who goes with the crowd, not who is meteorologically right. Data: groundhog-day.com.
